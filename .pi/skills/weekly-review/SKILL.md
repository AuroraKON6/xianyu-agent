# Weekly Review

Call `xianyu_business_snapshot` first. Separate system readiness from business
performance. Report candidates, approvals, published listings, inquiries, paid
orders, fulfillment, after-sales and actual profit as distinct stages.

Name one primary bottleneck and one executable next action. Exposure without
inquiries suggests listing or offer work; inquiries without payment suggests
price, trust, specification or delivery friction; paid orders without reliable
fulfillment block scaling; revenue without reconciled profit is not success.

Decide continue, improve, pause or stop from observed evidence. Do not advise
adding products or accounts before the one-item loop is complete.

Detailed template: `docs/ops/opc/weekly-review.md`.
