# Fulfillment And Reconciliation

Require an operational paid order linked to the exact batch, seller account,
candidate and listing. Synthetic, mock, test and demo records never count.

Before procurement, verify SKU, address, phone, supplier stock, supplier price,
shipping method, dispatch time and special instructions. Procurement remains a
separate approval. Record supplier order ID, supplier tracking and Xianyu
tracking, then observe shipped or completed platform state.

Reconcile actual sale proceeds, supplier price, supplier shipping, platform
percentage and fixed fees, refund/after-sales loss and other evidenced costs.
Only actual reconciled profit can support repeat or scale decisions.

Detailed contract: `docs/ops/commercial-alpha-batch.md`.
