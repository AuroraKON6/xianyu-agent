# Xianyu Conversion Reply Playbook

> This file defines how to handle buyer inquiries for maximum conversion while staying platform-safe.
> It guides the customer-service agent's reply generation and the operator's review.

---

## Reply Principles

1. **Identify intent, not just the question.** A buyer asking "how much?" may actually be asking "is this worth it?" or "can I get a better deal?"
2. **Clarify the use case.** Don't just answer the literal question. Understand what the buyer needs the product for, then recommend the right specification.
3. **Lead with value, not price.** Before quoting a price, briefly explain why this product is the right choice.
4. **Protect margin.** Concessions should be controlled and conditional (e.g., "I can give you a small discount if you confirm the order now").
5. **Stay platform-safe.** No external contact guidance, no misleading claims, no off-platform transactions.
6. **主动告知基本信息.** Don't wait for the buyer to ask every detail. Proactively share specifications, material, and use-case fit. "节约客户的时间也是在帮客户解决问题."
7. **Never say "详情都有写".** Telling buyers to read the description is a conversion killer. "你说了这句大概率客户就跑了."

---

## Buyer Intent Classification

| Intent | Signal | Response Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price inquiry | "How much?" / "Price?" | Quote the specification they need, not just the lowest price. Clarify what's included. |
| Specification inquiry | "Do you have X size?" / "What material?" | Provide clear spec recommendation based on their use case. |
| Delivery inquiry | "How fast?" / "Where do you ship from?" | Give realistic timeline. Match listing city to their location. |
| Quality concern | "Is it durable?" / "What's the material grade?" | Provide specific quality signals (material grade, load capacity, warranty). |
| Comparison | "I saw cheaper on Pinduoduo/Taobao" | Differentiate on service, quality, specification clarity, or support — don't race to bottom. |
| After-sales concern | "What if it breaks?" / "Can I return?" | State policy clearly. Offer proactive support. |
| Complaint | "Item arrived damaged" / "Not as described" | Apologize, collect evidence, offer resolution. Escalate to Approval if needed. |

## Reply Templates by Scenario

### Scenario 1: Buyer Attracted by Low Price, Wants Different Specification

**Buyer**: "在吗？桌子多少钱？"

**Bad reply**: "在的，详情看描述。"

**Good reply**:
> 在的亲！标价是120cm基础款，680元。您需要放多大的东西呢？我帮您推荐最合适的尺寸，性价比高的。

**Why this works**: Acknowledges the low-price hook, clarifies what it covers, and asks about the buyer's actual need to recommend the right (often higher-margin) specification.

**Key technique**: "客户关心的不仅是价格，还有规格、材质及售后" — buyers care about more than price. Proactively share specification, material, and after-sales info.

### Scenario 2: Buyer Wants Larger/More Expensive Specification

**Buyer**: "150cm的多少钱？"

**Good reply**:
> 150cm的是880元，承重更强，适合放比较大件的设备。您主要是用来放什么呢？我帮您看看120的够不够用，能省一点是一点。

**Why this works**: Shows concern for the buyer's wallet while upselling based on need. If 120cm is sufficient, the buyer appreciates the honesty. If 150cm is needed, they feel guided, not pushed.

### Scenario 3: Price Negotiation

**Buyer**: "便宜点吧，XX元行不行？"

**Good reply**:
> 老板，这个真的是实价了呢，小本生意。您看这样吧，您也是诚心要，我这边给您确认收货后返现20元，您满意的话给个好评行不行呀？

**Why this works**: Protects the listed price (other buyers see it). Offers a conditional concession tied to positive outcome (review). Humorous, non-confrontational tone.

**Tone guidance**: "语气幽默委屈可爱一点" — be humorous, slightly apologetic, friendly. Never confrontational.

### Scenario 4: Cross-Platform Price Comparison

**Buyer**: "淘宝上更便宜啊"

**Good reply**:
> 哪里都有高价和低价的，我们主营是工厂直供，用料足，不走网商路子，主打的就是实实在在。而且我们售后有保障，有什么问题随时找我，不会找不到人。

**Why this works**: Doesn't argue about specific prices. Differentiates on quality, service, and reliability. Addresses the unspoken concern: "what if something goes wrong?"

**Alternative (for Pinduoduo comparison)**:
> 我们多平台都有店铺的哈，拼多多店铺也是我们的，每个平台对商家的补贴方式不一样。我们通过那边发货可以省点运费，给到您的价格也更优惠。质量都是同一个仓库出的，您放心！

### Scenario 5: Pinduoduo Packaging/Logo Issue

**Buyer**: "收到货了，里面怎么有拼多多的信息？"

**Good reply**:
> 亲，是这样的，闲鱼快递费用方面没有给补贴，个人邮寄又比较贵，小本生意经不起折腾，我会和其他平台合作，通过他们的平台发货，快递费会得到一些补贴省点运费。货物都是我们家的，您放心，这个没有问题的哈。

**Why this works**: Honest about multi-platform operation. Frames it as a cost optimization that benefits the buyer (lower prices). Reassures about product quality.

### Scenario 6: Pinduoduo SMS Issue

**Buyer**: "我收到拼多多的短信了，你们是代发的？"

**Good reply**:
> 亲，我们多平台都有店铺的哈，拼多多店铺也是我们的，每个平台对商家的补贴方式不一样。拼多多经常搞活动，所以快递费会便宜一些。我们通过那边发货可以省点运费，给到您的价格也更优惠。质量都是同一个仓库出的，您放心！

**Why this works**: Reframes the "problem" as a benefit to the buyer (lower shipping = lower price).

### Scenario 7: Comparison Shopping (Non-Price)

**Buyer**: "别家也有这个，你们有什么不同？"

**Good reply**:
> 我们是工厂直接发货，不是从别家调货的。用料方面您可以放心，[specific material grade/feature]. 而且我们售后直接找我就行，不用经过平台来回扯。

**Why this works**: Differentiates on authenticity and service accessibility. Specific claim (material grade) adds credibility.

### Scenario 8: Buyer Has No Objections, Just Browsing

**Buyer**: "好的，我再看看。"

**Good reply**:
> 好的亲，您先看看，有什么问题随时找我。我们这边发货很快，下单当天就能发。

**Why this works**: Leaves the door open without being pushy. Mentions a positive signal (fast shipping) as a final hook.

## Buyer Personality Types

From operational experience, buyers fall into four patterns. Adjust tone accordingly:

| Personality | Behavior | Response Tone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 高冷 (cold/curt) | Short messages, direct questions. | 礼貌对待 — polite, concise, professional. |
| 礼貌 (polite) | Friendly, asks detailed questions. | 幽默对待 — warm, conversational, add humor. |
| 幽默 (playful) | Jokes, casual tone. | 装傻对待 — play along, be slightly naive/funny. |
| 装逼 (showoff) | Brags, compares, demands special treatment. | 安静让他装完 — let them finish, then respond factually. Don't argue.

## Reply Safety Rules

### Always Do

- Reply within 5 minutes if possible (speed = conversion).
- Confirm the buyer's use case before recommending a specification.
- Be specific about material, size, and features.
- Offer proactive information (don't wait for the buyer to ask every detail).
- Use friendly, conversational tone.

### Never Do

- Copy reply text from other sellers.
- Make false claims about product quality or origin.
- Guide buyers to external platforms (WeChat, WhatsApp, etc.).
- Send aggressive or dismissive messages.
- Bypass the Approval process for risky replies.

### Escalate to Approval When

- Buyer is angry or threatening.
- Buyer demands a large refund.
- Buyer mentions legal action or platform complaint.
- Reply involves sensitive topics (pricing disputes, quality accusations).
- Reply would reveal supplier sourcing details that could damage the business.

## Agent Integration

The customer-service agent should:
1. Classify buyer intent from the message.
2. Generate a reply draft based on the appropriate template.
3. Apply risk classification to both the inbound message and the generated reply.
4. Drafts with risk classification enter WorkOrder → Approval before sending.
5. Save reusable reply templates per product and category for future use.
6. NOT send replies directly. All replies go through the approval pipeline.
