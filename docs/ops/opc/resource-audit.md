# OPC Resource Audit

> This file inventories the founder's available resources for the Xianyu one-person company operation.
> It is a reference document, not runtime code. Agents and dashboards use it to ground decisions in reality.

---

## 1. Accounts and Platforms

- **Xianyu account**: Active, with existing listing history and buyer reputation.
- **Pinduoduo account**: Primary sourcing platform. Established relationships with 5+ suppliers, 2 long-term partners offering factory-direct pricing.
- **Feishu**: Connected via Feishu CLI long-connection for event intake and operator notifications.
- **OpenClaw**: Control layer for routing instructions to Xianyu and ecommerce projects.

## 2. Supplier Relationships

- **Pinduoduo suppliers**: 5+ active suppliers. 2 long-term with factory-direct pricing (below Pinduoduo retail by several hundred yuan per unit).
- **Direct manufacturer contacts**: Growing network. Strategy is to approach small factories on Pinduoduo who self-operate their stores and negotiate bulk/team pricing.
- **Negotiation method**: Offer to drive team sales from Xianyu; start with lower margin to prove volume, then negotiate better terms.
- **Supplier pitch script**: "我们本身是有团队的，直接告诉他，我就是在闲鱼卖，可以发动小伙伴一起去帮他卖。" Small factories on Pinduoduo who self-operate are easiest to negotiate — they have good products but need sales volume.
- **Proven result**: 平冷操作台 (cold-storage work table) — obtained factory-direct pricing several hundred yuan below Pinduoduo retail. One account could sell 4-5 units per cycle, 200+ yuan profit per unit. Category generated significant revenue over 3-4 months.
- **Pinduoduo issue mitigation**: Supplier cooperation for "无痕发货" (neutral packaging), no price lists or好评返现 cards, no SMS notifications. If Pinduoduo sends SMS, reframe as "multi-platform operation for lower shipping costs."

## 3. Skills and Capabilities

- **Xianyu operations**: Experienced. Proven track record with commercial kitchen equipment (cold-storage work tables, beverage cabinets). Single product category generated significant revenue over 3-4 months.
- **Product selection**: Daily practice. Uses raw-material expansion method, high-ticket scanning, batch-demand scanning. Comfortable with cold/niche categories.
- **Copywriting**: Can write differentiated titles, descriptions, and trust-building copy. Understands the "non-standard products compete on detail" principle.
- **Negotiation**: Skilled at buyer conversion, price anchoring, specification guidance, and handling objections.
- **Platform navigation**: Understands Xianyu algorithm, exposure mechanics, listing optimization, and risk boundaries.

## 4. Content and Assets

- **Listing templates**: Reusable patterns for commercial kitchen equipment, wood products, stainless steel goods.
- **Reply templates**: Tested buyer-conversion scripts for specification questions, price negotiation, cross-platform comparison, and post-sale issues.
- **Image approach**: Scene-based product images, not copied from other sellers. Uses real product photos and application场景 shots.
- **Category playbooks**: Documented selection methods (原材料选品法, 高客单价扫描, 批量需求扫描), listing strategies (低价引流三法), conversion scripts (四种买家类型应对), and after-sales procedures.
- **Asset compounding principle**: Save every successful title pattern, reply template, supplier note, and category observation. These compound over time — one good template serves hundreds of listings.

## 5. Channels

- **Xianyu**: Primary sales channel. Monthly active users 200M+. Organic traffic only (no paid ads).
- **Pinduoduo**: Primary sourcing channel. Also serves as a price reference and backup fulfillment path.
- **Feishu**: Operator notification and approval channel.
- **Future**: Potential expansion to other Xianyu accounts (vertical stores), or cross-border ecommerce bridge.

## 6. Constraints

- **Time**: One-person operation. Must prioritize high-leverage activities (selection, listing optimization, conversion) over low-leverage ones (manual customer service, logistics tracking).
- **Cash**: Low startup cost. Xianyu requires no deposit. Pinduoduo allows 0-yuan orders on some items. Main cash risk is large orders requiring upfront payment to supplier.
- **Bandwidth**: Cannot operate unlimited categories simultaneously. Focus on 1-2 verticals + 1 miscellaneous store.
- **Psychological constraint**: Must manage expectations. Not every inquiry converts. Not every listing gets traction. High exposure ≠ high profit (electronics get millions of views but margins are thin; commercial equipment gets thousands but margins are 200+ per unit). Focus on what's controllable: selection, title, image, copy, reply quality.

## 7. Hard Boundaries

- No Ollama. No captcha bypass. No platform risk-control evasion.
- No auto-pay, auto-refund, or auto-supplier-ordering.
- No copying third-party images or seller copy as owned material.
- High-risk actions (publish, message, fulfill, refund, delete) require WorkOrder + Approval + ExecutionJobQueue.
